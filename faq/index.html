Frequently asked questions — used & new heavy machinery export from China

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. (Hongzhitai / HZT) is a Guangxi-based exporter of used and new heavy machinery, including concrete pump trucks, wheel loaders, excavators, and rotary drilling rigs. Brands we commonly work with include SANY, Zoomlion, LiuGong, and XCMG. Below are practical answers for overseas contractors and dealers.

What does Hongzhitai export?

Used and new heavy machinery from China: concrete pump trucks, wheel loaders, excavators, and rotary drilling rigs. Exact units change with inventory — ask for the current list rather than relying on old screenshots.

Which brands do you handle?

Public site references SANY, Zoomlion, LiuGong, and XCMG. Availability by brand depends on what is in stock or sourcable at the time of inquiry.

Do you sell used concrete pump trucks?

Yes — used (and new) concrete pump trucks are a core category. For boom length, year, hours, and chassis, request current photos/specs for the specific unit; we do not publish fictional stock lists.

Can buyers outside China purchase from you?

Yes. We serve overseas contractors and dealers. Shipping terms (for example FOB or CIF) and documents are confirmed per deal — ask for a written quotation for your destination port.

Can I inspect a machine before buying?

Inspection options (yard visit, third-party inspector, or video walk-around) can be arranged case by case. Say which unit you mean and which inspection method you prefer.

What information should I send in a first inquiry?

Target machine type (e.g. used boom pump), preferred brand if any, destination country/port, budget range if you have one, and timeline. WhatsApp: +86 135 5771 6777 or use the contact form on hongzhtaichina.com.

Do you invent or guarantee always-in-stock models?

No. Inventory moves. Any listing should match a real unit with photos and specs. If a page shows a machine, treat specs on that page as the source of truth for that unit only.

Where is the company based?

Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, China. Legal name: Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd.

How do I contact Hongzhitai?

WhatsApp +86 135 5771 6777, or the contact section on https://www.hongzhtaichina.com/.
