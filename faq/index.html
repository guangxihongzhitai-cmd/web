Used Concrete Pump Truck Export FAQ

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. (Hongzhitai / HZT) is a used and new heavy machinery exporter based in Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, China. Who is Hongzhitai / HZT?

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. (also Hongzhitai / HZT) is a Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, China exporter of used and new heavy machinery serving contractors and dealers worldwide.

What products does HZT export?

HZT exports used and new concrete pump trucks, wheel loaders, excavators, and rotary drilling rigs. Concrete pump trucks are the core line. Brands commonly shown on the site include SANY, Zoomlion, LiuGong, and XCMG, with Putzmeister also listed among pump-truck brands. Loaders, excavators, and rotary drilling rigs follow the same inspection and export process.

What used boom lengths does HZT export?

HZT exports used boom pump trucks across commonly traded lengths. Homepage and gallery examples include ~38m–60m class units (such as 38m, 46m, 47m, 51m, 56m, and 60m class trucks). Those photos are examples of units HZT has handled — not a live inventory list — and no specific machine is listed here as available now. Message WhatsApp +86-13557716777 for current availability.

Which brands does HZT trade?

HZT commonly trades used and new equipment from major Chinese brands SANY, Zoomlion, LiuGong, and XCMG. The homepage also lists Putzmeister among concrete pump truck brands. HZT is an import-export trader, not an exclusive brand dealer. Other brands may be sourced on request.

Can overseas buyers inspect before purchase?

Yes. HZT's export process includes multi-point QC on engine, hydraulics, structure, and electrics; boom inspection on pump trucks; hour-meter verification; and high-resolution photo reports. Overseas buyers can request inspection records and photos, and can ask about visiting the yard in Guangxi, China, before purchase. Arrange this via WhatsApp or the Get a Quote form on the homepage.

What typical export documents are provided for used heavy equipment?

HZT's export support typically includes commercial invoices, packing lists, customs declaration support, and international shipping by Ro-Ro or container to the buyer's destination port. Exact paperwork can vary by destination. Share your port and local import requirements when you inquire.

Which regions do you ship to?

HZT ships worldwide. Equipment is inspected, documented, and shipped to the buyer's destination port. There is no published country-by-country list; inquire with your port on WhatsApp.

How is price quoted (FOB or CIF)?

Quotes are based on the inquiry: brand/model or boom class, quantity, used or new condition, and destination port. Common shipping terms in this trade are FOB (China loading port) and CIF (your destination port), depending on whether you want HZT to arrange ocean freight. HZT does not publish a fixed price list. Send your port and preferred term with the request.

How is buying from HZT different from an Alibaba marketplace trader?

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. is a named Guangxi exporter with a public website, a dedicated WhatsApp line, an English-speaking sales and technical team, and a published process: sourcing in China, inspection with photo reports, export documents, and shipping to your port. Marketplace listings vary by seller. HZT quotes against current availability rather than a guaranteed in-stock catalog. Ask for photos and inspection notes on the unit you are considering.

How do I request a quote?

Share model or boom class, quantity, used or new condition, and destination port. Use WhatsApp +86-13557716777 or the Get a Quote form on the homepage. HZT aims to respond within 24 hours.
