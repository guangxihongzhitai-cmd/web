About Us

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd.

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. is a Guangxi-based exporter of used and new heavy machinery serving contractors and dealers worldwide.

We specialize in concrete pump trucks from SANY, Zoomlion, LiuGong and XCMG, and have expanded into wheel loaders, excavators, and rotary drilling rigs — all with the same transparent inspection and export process.

  • Direct sourcing from factories, dealers and auctions across China
  • Full export documentation and port-to-port logistics
  • English-speaking sales and technical team
