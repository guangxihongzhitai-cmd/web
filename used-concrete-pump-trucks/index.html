Export Process

Inspection, documents, shipping

End-to-end export support as described on the HZT homepage — honest process, not a delivery guarantee for a named unit.

01

Select

Technical consultation to match boom class, brand, budget, and working conditions. Direct sourcing from factories, dealers, and auctions across China.

02

Inspect

Multi-point QC on engine, hydraulics, structure, and electrics; boom inspection; hour-meter verification; high-resolution photo reports. Overseas buyers can request records or ask about visiting the Guangxi yard.

03

Document

Commercial invoices, packing lists, and customs declaration support. Exact paperwork can vary by destination — share your port and import rules when you inquire.

04

Ship

Ro-Ro or container shipping to ports worldwide. Quotes are commonly discussed as FOB (China port) or CIF (your port). Remote guidance and spare-parts assistance are available after delivery.
