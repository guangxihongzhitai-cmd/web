Privacy Policy

This page says what we do with personal information you send us when you get in touch.

Who we are

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. (广西鸿之泰进出口贸易有限公司) runs this website.

Our address is No.53, 11th Team, Beihuaipo, Lexian Village, Jiangnan District, Nanning, Guangxi, China.

Contact email: sales@hongzhtaichina.com

What we collect

When you contact us through the website contact form or chat, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram messages, or email, we collect:

  • your name
  • contact details (email, phone or account handle)
  • the messages you send
  • any photos or files you send

Why we use it

We use this information to answer equipment inquiries, prepare quotes, arrange inspections, yard visits and shipping, and keep a record of the conversation.

We do not sell personal data

We do not sell personal data, and we don't use it for unrelated advertising.

Other companies that process data

These companies process data as part of these services: Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp), Cloudflare (website hosting and security), GitHub Pages (hosting), and our email provider.

We share information with a shipping or inspection partner only when that is needed for a deal you asked for.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry records as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any resulting deal, then delete them, generally within 24 months of last contact unless the law requires longer.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

How to delete your data

Email sales@hongzhtaichina.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name or account you used. We confirm and delete the data within 30 days.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies.

Changes to this policy

If how we handle personal information changes, we will update this page.
