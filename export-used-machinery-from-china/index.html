Export Used Machinery from China

Guangxi Hongzhitai Import & Export Trading Co., Ltd. (Hongzhitai / HZT) exports used and new heavy machinery from Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region, China. Export support covers inspection, documentation, and international shipping to the buyer's destination port — the same process described on the HZT homepage, without a delivery guarantee for a named unit until that unit is confirmed.

Equipment lines include concrete pump trucks, wheel loaders, excavators, and rotary drilling rigs, commonly from SANY, Zoomlion, LiuGong, and XCMG. Share model or class, used or new, and destination port on WhatsApp +86-13557716777 or via Get a Quote.

How Export Works

Inspection, documents, shipping

Honest process language from HZT's public services copy — not a promise that a specific machine is booked, certified, or already on the water.

01

Technical Support

Professional technical consultation. HZT helps you select a suitable model based on project requirements, budget, and working conditions. Sourcing is from factories, dealers, and auctions across China.

02

Inspection

Multi-point QC on engine, hydraulics, structure, and electrics; boom inspection on pump trucks; hour-meter verification; high-resolution photo reports. Overseas buyers can request records and can ask about visiting the yard in Guangxi before purchase.

03

Documents

Export support typically includes commercial invoices, packing lists, and customs declaration support. Exact paperwork can vary by destination. Share your port and local import requirements when you inquire.

04

Logistics & Customs

Complete export service: inspection, container loading, customs declaration, and international shipping — Ro-Ro or container — to the destination port. HZT ships worldwide; there is no published country-by-country list.

05

Overseas Support

Remote technical guidance, operation support, and spare-parts assistance are available for overseas customers after delivery. OEM and aftermarket parts and operator guidance help keep the fleet productive after arrival.

06

Quoting

Quotes use the inquiry: brand/model or class, quantity, used or new, and destination port. Common terms are FOB (China loading port) and CIF (your destination port). HZT does not publish a fixed price list.
